# Lumen Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Lumen is a community project, and we want participation to be welcoming,
respectful, and safe. We commit to providing an environment free from
harassment and discrimination regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, race, religion,
sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or any other protected or personal
characteristic.

This code applies to contributors, maintainers, reviewers, users, and anyone
representing Lumen.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that support our community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and respect;
- assuming good faith while asking for clarification when needed;
- giving and receiving constructive, specific technical feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and learning from them;
- respecting different backgrounds, abilities, opinions, and experiences;
- focusing criticism on code, ideas, and behaviour rather than people; and
- respecting privacy and not sharing information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks;
- sexualised language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or deliberate disruption;
- publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information;
- stalking, unwanted contact, or continued contact after being asked to stop;
- impersonation, doxxing, or malicious disclosure of security information; and
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or public technical community.

Technical disagreement is welcome. It must remain relevant, constructive, and
respectful.

## Project spaces and scope

This code applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, code,
documentation, chat, meetings, and other spaces used for Lumen. It also applies
when a person is representing Lumen in public, including through a project
account, official event, or project-branded communication.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content that violates this code and may
restrict participation temporarily or permanently when necessary to protect the
community. They will aim to act fairly, proportionately, and consistently.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
Lumen maintainers through a private GitHub channel. Do not include sensitive
information in a public issue. If you cannot find a private contact route, open
a non-sensitive issue asking the maintainers to provide one.

For a report, include what happened, where and when it happened, the people
involved, and any evidence you can safely share. Please remove passwords,
private keys, personal addresses, and other unnecessary personal information.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
Maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who makes a good-faith report.
They may contact the reporter for clarification, explain the outcome where
appropriate, and take action based on the available evidence. Confidentiality
cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required by law or necessary to
protect someone from immediate harm.

## False or bad-faith reports

Reports made in good faith are welcome even when an investigation does not
substantiate them. Deliberately fabricated reports, retaliation, or misuse of
the reporting process may themselves violate this code.

## Acknowledgement

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and tailored to Lumen's
GitHub-based development workflow. The project follows the version committed
in this repository.
